# Contributing to AIM

Thank you for considering a contribution to the AIM (AI Impact and
Momentum) public materials.

At this stage, the repository is primarily a published reference
release. Suggestions, corrections and issue reports are welcome, but
please contact the repository maintainer before preparing a substantial
contribution or derivative work for inclusion.

## How to suggest a correction or improvement

1.  Open an issue in the repository, if issues are enabled, or use the
    contact route specified in the repository.
2.  Identify the relevant file, section, question, KPI or template.
3.  Describe the proposed change and the reason for it.
4.  Where possible, distinguish a factual correction from a suggested
    methodological change.

Please do not include confidential, personal or commercially sensitive
information in issues or proposed examples.

## Contributions and licensing

Do not submit a contribution on the assumption that it will
automatically become part of AIM or be accepted into the official
release.

Before a contribution is incorporated, the maintainer may ask the
contributor to confirm that they have the right to submit it and to
agree to the applicable terms for inclusion and distribution. The public
licence applying to existing AIM materials does not, by itself,
determine ownership or licensing terms for new contributions.

## Scope and maintainership

The AIM creator or authorised repository maintainer determines whether
suggestions are accepted, how the methodology is maintained, and when
official releases are issued. A suggestion or contribution does not
imply endorsement or affiliation.
